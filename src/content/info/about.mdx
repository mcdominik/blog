# Dominik Mackiewicz — the person behind Dominik Builds

I'm Dominik Mackiewicz, a software engineer and open-source enthusiast. **Dominik Builds** is the name of this blog and my [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@DominikBuilds). On [GitHub, I'm mcdominik](https://github.com/mcdominik).

Here I write about JavaScript and TypeScript, database design, and electronics. My programming articles cover decisions such as [choosing an identifier format](/posts/which-uuid-is-better/) and [storing monetary values](/posts/store-monetary-values/). My Arduino tutorials combine wiring diagrams, code examples, and videos, including guides to [OLED displays](/posts/arduino-oled-display/) and [servo motors](/posts/arduino-servo-motors/).

You can find the example sketches and bitmap conversion script for the OLED tutorial in my [project repository](https://github.com/mcdominik/source/tree/main/arduino/oled_tutorial), and the code for this blog in its [source repository](https://github.com/mcdominik/blog).

I believe that sharing knowledge is one of the best ways to grow, both as an individual and as a community.

My main areas of interest are programming and electronics. I do my best to verify everything I publish, but as you probably know, bugs and mistakes are an inevitable part of building anything worthwhile. If you spot an error or something inaccurate on this site, please let me know. I’m always open to learning and updating my views when presented with better information.
